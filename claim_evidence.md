# Claim-to-evidence map

| ID | Claim and scope | Evidence | Status / boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Bounded continuous scalar parameterization alone does not imply VC dimension at most two | Manuscript construction, common-threshold shattering; exhaustive tests through K=6; pilot eight-mask output | Constructive proof. An elementary capacity fact, not claimed as a new general principle. |
| C2 | The constructed family is C-infinity, bounded in [0,1], 1-Lipschitz in lambda, and atomless at every fixed lambda | Equation (4)/(5) and Appendix A derivative bound, mixture-of-uniforms argument | Analytical proof; simulations cannot certify smoothness. Domain U in [0,1], labels finite. |
| C3 | Conditional adaptive coverage equals distinct-label fraction times the kth U order statistic | Theorem 1 proof; src/core.py, scalar prediction test; pilot zero disagreements in 20,000 predictions | Exact mathematical event; direct scalar numeric implementation checked only for small K. |
| C4 | Marginal coverage equals c_n[1-(1-1/K)^n] for k<=n | Theorem 1 and occupancy independence calculation; exact rational occupancy enumeration; 21 confirmatory settings | Proven; each fixed K is a fixed family. k=n+1 is explicitly excluded and evaluated with infinity. |
| C5 | No rate depending only on n, dimension, bounded range and raw Lipschitz constant is uniformly vanishing over the constructed families | Theorem 1 and inequality coverage<=c_n*n/K | Proven across families. Does not assert permanent failure of one fixed family or of ordinary temperature scaling. |
| C6 | Rank covers have size at least binomial(K,floor(K/2)) at small constant radius | Proposition 3 and explicit population rank formulas for equal-size subsets | Separation proof; not a measured entropy estimate. |
| C7 | Rank-cover beta-tail lower/upper certificates hold for arbitrary measurable selectors | Theorem 2, order-statistic perturbation inequality, classical beta law, union bound | Proof under iid data, atomless fixed scores and deterministic common-domain covers. No independence among centers assumed. |
| C8 | Uniform score Lipschitzness and anti-concentration yield rank-cover radius omega(2L epsilon), at most 2BL epsilon for bounded densities | Event inclusions in Section 5.1 | Proof; B or omega are population quantities. No validity claim for plug-in estimates. |
| C9 | Every fixed compact bounded uniformly Lipschitz family with atomless scores has asymptotically nominal conditional coverage for arbitrary selectors | Corollary 4 and Appendix B compactness proof plus reduction to Newey and McFadden (1994), Lemma 2.4 | Classical consequence, explicitly attributed. Holds under even weaker pointwise continuity; not claimed as a new theorem. Fixed family/distribution, no dimension-only uniform rate. |
| C10 | Severe case coverage is about .008863, with exact .00886648; independent test audit agrees | results/confirmatory/occupancy_summary.csv, raw file occupancy_n99_K10000_a0.1.npz; independent_test_audit.json/.npz | Executed, 10,000 calibration samples plus independent 1,000x1,000 audit. Large masks evaluate an equivalent mathematical representation. |
| C11 | Fixed, monotone and disjoint split controls recover their appropriate order-statistic targets | All 84 rows of occupancy_summary.csv; exact fixed-score theorem, independence and invariance | Executed; matched total n. Smaller split calibration size changes the finite-sample target. |
| C12 | Continuum certificate is conservative and does not uniformly dominate finite-grid beta certificate in regular-family tests | linear_summary.csv, raw arrays, exact convolution CDF, Figure 2 | Executed in six synthetic settings. Certificate comparisons are not comparisons of prediction algorithms. |
| C13 | Main implementation checks pass | logs/tests.txt; results/revision1_reproduction/tests.txt and comparison.json; src/test_core.py | Six initial tests, eight after review edge-case fixes. Unchanged confirmatory rerun differs by at most 9.55e-15. Finite checks supplement, never replace, proofs. Reviewer B preserves additional independent checks. |
| C14 | Runtime of the confirmatory script is 10.88 seconds on inspected local hardware | results/confirmatory/run_manifest.json, logs/confirmatory_run.txt | Measured for this script only; excludes setup, research, figures and writing. No efficiency claim. |
