# Readiness assessment

**Classification: complete executed research draft, suitable for external expert critique; not ready for ICML submission.** This is more than a proposal or an unexecuted outline. Proofs, synthetic experiments, primary-source positioning, manuscript and reproducible artifacts have been produced. The remaining blocker to a strong full-paper recommendation is novelty and significance, not a missing run of the specified experiments.

The defensible contribution is a smooth, bounded, scalar split-conformal counterexample with atomless score distributions and an exact occupancy law, together with a population-rank covering specialization. It directly exposes a missing structural assumption in a published dimension argument. Parameter count versus VC complexity and severe non-monotone adaptation failures are established knowledge. Fixed-family consistency is a classical uniform-convergence consequence and is explicitly not claimed as new.

The research verifies a worst-case phenomenon. It does not show that ordinary temperature scaling fails, that the construction is computationally practical, or that the population certificate can be safely estimated from finite data. The scalar encodes exponentially many alternatives and requires increasing precision. Large-label experiments use an equivalent mask representation. The regular-family bounds are conservative and do not uniformly improve on a finite-grid beta bound.
