410
      We make the compactness step explicit. If ω(w) ̸→ 0, choose η > 0, wj ↓ 0, parameters λj and intervals (tj , tj + wj ] with
411
      probability at least η. Since scores lie in [0, 1], only interval locations in a bounded region can have positive probability for
412
      small wj . Pass to a subsequence with λj → λ∗ and tj → t∗ . On the interval event,
413
414                                    |Sλ∗ (Z) − t∗ | ≤ L∥λj − λ∗ ∥ + wj + |tj − t∗ | =: ej → 0.                                 (22)
415
      For any fixed e > 0, all sufficiently large j imply P(|Sλ∗ (Z) − t∗ | ≤ e) ≥ η. Letting e ↓ 0 gives an atom of mass at least η,
416
      a contradiction.
417
418   Thus for any r > 0, a sufficiently small parameter radius gives a rank net with error at most r. Its cardinality is finite and
419   does not depend on n. For every center, the beta mean is cn → 1 − α and its variance is k(n + 1 − k)/[(n + 1)2 (n + 2)] → 0.
420   A union bound over this fixed finite set gives convergence of both its maximum and minimum order statistics. This justifies
421   the bracket argument without requiring a rate for the concentration modulus. It also shows why allowing K and therefore
422   the score family to change with n falls outside the corollary.
