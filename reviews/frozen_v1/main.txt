413   We make the compactness step explicit. If ω(w) ̸→ 0, choose η > 0, wj ↓ 0, parameters λj and intervals (tj , tj + wj ] with

416

419   For any fixed e > 0, all sufficiently large j imply P(|Sλ∗ (Z) − t∗ | ≤ e) ≥ η. Letting e ↓ 0 gives an atom of mass at least η,
420   a contradiction.
